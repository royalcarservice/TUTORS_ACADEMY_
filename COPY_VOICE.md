# The Copy Voice — Tutors Academy homepage

Committed in Phase 4 · Step 1. Every later scene step writes against this document.
Machine-readable twin: `src/lib/spine/voice.ts`.

## The voice

- **Confident and plainspoken.** Speak to an intelligent person, not a customer.
- **Concrete over abstract.** Name the thing. Show the detail.
- **Adult.** No exclamation marks, no urgency, no hype.
- **Short sentences carry the page.** Long sentences earn their length or get cut.
- **Second person, addressed to the student.** "You" means the learner.

## Claims

- Every claim must be demonstrable in the product. If a capability is not built,
  it is described in words inside the one honest roadmap scene, or not shown.
- No claim about outcomes the product does not control (marks, ranks, admissions).
- No testimonial, statistic or credential may be invented. Not even a
  plausible-sounding one as a placeholder. Scenes that need one render an honest
  empty state until real material exists.

## Banned outright

unlock your potential · revolutionize · transform your future · game-changing ·
world-class · cutting-edge · seamless · empower · journey (as a marketing noun) ·
next-generation · leverage · elevate · discover the magic · unleash ·
"in today's fast-paced world" · rhetorical questions as headlines · triple stacks
of adjectives · em-dash-heavy listicles.

Any existing occurrence must be flagged in the step report, not silently shipped.
